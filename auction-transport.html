FAQ

Common questions.

How do I get a price?

Fill out the quote form or call dispatch at (732) 877-9950. We review your route, vehicle and timing, then contact you with pricing. Quote requests are no-obligation.

What information do you need?

Pickup and delivery ZIP codes, the year, make and model of each vehicle, the number of vehicles, whether the vehicle runs, your estimated pickup date and your contact details.

Do you move inoperable vehicles?

Tell us the vehicle condition in the form and dispatch will confirm whether we can handle your move.

How many vehicles fit on one load?

Our carrier holds up to five vehicles on one load, depending on vehicle size.

Do you serve my area?

We take transport requests nationwide across the lower 48 states. Dispatch confirms availability for each route.

How is pickup scheduled?

After you approve your quote, dispatch coordinates a pickup window with you or your contact at the dealership, auction or terminal, and keeps you updated through delivery.
